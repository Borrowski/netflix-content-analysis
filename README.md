# Netflix Content Analysis: Exploring Trends, Genres and Global Distribution

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the Netflix Movies and TV Shows catalogue using Python to identify patterns in content type, growth over time, production countries, content ratings, duration and genres.

The goal was to take a raw dataset through the data analysis process — cleaning and transforming the data, performing exploratory data analysis (EDA), creating visualisations and translating the findings into business insights.

## 🎯 Business Question

How is Netflix's content catalogue distributed across content types, countries, ratings and genres, and what opportunities can be identified from these patterns?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The dataset was prepared for analysis by:

- Handling missing categorical values
- Removing records where missing values could not reasonably be estimated
- Converting date fields to appropriate datetime formats
- Creating a `year_added` feature for trend analysis
- Separating duration into numeric values and units
- Renaming columns for improved readability
- Preparing genre data for analysis

## 📊 Analysis Performed

The project examines:

- Movies vs TV Shows
- Titles added to Netflix over time
- Top content-producing countries
- Content ratings by content type
- Movie duration distribution
- Genre distribution across Movies and TV Shows

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Movies account for approximately 70% of the analysed Netflix catalogue.
- Content additions accelerated significantly after 2015 and peaked around 2019.
- The United States is the largest content-producing country in the dataset, followed by India and the United Kingdom.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating across Movies and TV Shows.
- Most movies are concentrated around typical feature-length running times.
- International Movies, Dramas and Comedies are among the strongest movie genres, while TV content is more concentrated in International TV Shows and TV Dramas.

## 💡 Business Recommendation

The analysis suggests an opportunity to further diversify Netflix's catalogue by expanding content from underrepresented countries, genres and audience segments.

Increasing investment in regional, family-oriented and internationally diverse content could broaden audience reach while reducing concentration around the catalogue's dominant markets and content categories.
